## Concrete policy attribution and design rationale

For the saved `token_only` control input `answer="False"`, reference `"True"`, the literal-token guard accepts `"False"` and the native reward is 1.0. The [P84/P91 witness rows](experiments/reward-contract-controlled-v1/results/comparison/witnesses.json) attribute that identical return differently: P84's preserved `[valid_boolean_token, equals_reference]` violates equality, whereas P91's `[valid_boolean_token]` has no violation for this witness because P91 explicitly permits either label. Rules abstain on both. Separately, P91's saved [global NoAlarm certificate](experiments/reward-contract-controlled-v1/results/comparison/globals.json) checks that all normal full-credit path guards imply validity; the single native return is not the proof. This uses the same source/input as the report, and the [exact saved native record](experiments/reward-contract-controlled-v1/results/comparison/native/native-8ad24ebb66adfa344a08.json) is unchanged.

The fixed-V control's four-cell deficit (20/24 versus the LLM's 24/24) repeats this one E/token-only semantic-source distinction across four E descriptions. U abstention is a separate administrative benefit, excluded from binary accuracy. The distinct truthiness-source wrong-witness example remains relevant to fixed E: a mismatched but valid Boolean token is allowed by V even though another non-Boolean full-credit candidate establishes a genuine global V violation.

This illustrates the design choices: preserve omissions in the explicit IR, hold the semantic engine fixed, validate the specific returned witness against the designed policy, and preserve Unknown for missing contracts or unsupported semantics. Necessary-condition certificates do not establish complete policy correctness or rule out under-rewarding. E and E+V have the same evaluation class under canonical Boolean references, but raw selected lists remain distinct and unchanged.

The relevant recorded replay comparisons are retained alongside each stage. They establish repeated execution consistency under the recorded method/environment, not an increase in independent sample size. The final closeout did not rerun them.
